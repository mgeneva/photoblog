When you are in the Pyrenees in the summer, your plans are very much dictated by the local weather cycle. It typically begins with a few hot and humid days, which lead to a storm, followed by a period of cold, gray weather—after which the cycle repeats.

During our week near Lourdes, we took advantage of the sunny days to go on two glorious hikes.
The first was in the Parc national des Pyrénées. We drove to the village of Gavarnie and followed a trail that led to the breathtaking Cirque de Gavarnie.

After the inevitable rainstorm and stretch of cool weather had passed, we set out on our second hike in the Néouvielle massif under bright, sunny skies. We spent a night at the Aygues-Cluses mountain refuge. The highlight was climbing Pic de Madamète, where we were rewarded with sweeping views of numerous lakes and the distant mountain range.
